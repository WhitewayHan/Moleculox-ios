MOLECULOX iOS V8.5.64 R19 — FINAL STATIC VALIDATION
====================================================

Scope
-----
- iOS only. Web and Android packages were not modified.
- Base: approved Moleculox iOS V8.5.63 R18.
- R18 Dr. E size/position, board position, 25-line voice sprite, player isolation,
  Apple nonce, Google/email authentication, Firebase/cloud sync and level data are retained.

R19 menu voice repair
---------------------
- The first studio/boot-logo gesture only unlocks and preloads audio; it does not enqueue speech.
- Main-menu speech is scheduled only after the splash/main-menu screen is visible and the screen
  transition has completed.
- Fresh automatic guests are not classified as returning players merely because tutorialDone=true.
- Fresh players use neutral existing menu recordings; established players use the recorded
  “Welcome back, Professor!” line; returning from gameplay uses a neutral home-menu bank.
- Voice queue entries now retain their event type, so stale delayed menu greetings can be removed
  when the player leaves the menu without cutting an active spoken line.
- R18’s one-at-a-time non-cutting voice queue and single local audio sprite remain unchanged.

R19 tutorial GOAL repair
------------------------
- The tutorial no longer measures GOAL while the game screen is hidden or still resizing.
- It waits for two animation frames, performs the game resize, and waits for the screen transition.
- The spotlight is measured from the live GOAL card bounding rectangle.
- Alignment is repeated after 180 ms, 380 ms and 720 ms to cover delayed iOS layout settling.
- The spotlight re-aligns on window/visualViewport resize and stale alignment timers are invalidated
  when the tutorial advances or closes.
