2. The game

Moleculox is a chemistry puzzle game. Campaign play works offline. Cloud save and online features require an internet connection. The World Ranking can be viewed by guests, but publishing an entry requires a permanent Google or email/password account.

3. Friendly online Atom Duels

Two-phone Duels are friendly and unranked. Players may join by a six-digit room code or use Quick Match. Opponent moves, round state and preset messages are synchronized through Firebase. Free-text chat is not provided. If a player disconnects, the game may pause for a reconnect grace period and then award a forfeit result or cancel the match according to the connection state.

4. Fair play

The free World Ranking is generated from progress submitted by the game client and protected by Firebase identity, type, range and monotonic-progress rules. It is not equivalent to fully server-validated competitive scoring. Fabricating results, exploiting bugs, manipulating the ranking or deliberately disrupting online matches is prohibited and may result in removal of an entry or account.

7. Availability

Online features depend on third-party infrastructure, network quality and platform services and may occasionally be unavailable. Offline campaign play is not affected by a Firebase outage after the app shell has been installed successfully.
