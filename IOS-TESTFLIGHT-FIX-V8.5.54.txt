MOLECULOX iOS V8.5.54 — TESTFLIGHT FIX

Fixed from real TestFlight evidence:
- Google, Apple and email sign-in no longer wait indefinitely for guest-auth restoration.
- Native WKWebView creates Firebase Auth with browser-local persistence at initialization, avoiding a background setPersistence race during explicit sign-in.
- Anonymous auth applies its returned user immediately when WKWebView delays the auth listener.
- Native Google/Apple ID tokens are exchanged with Firebase through the existing credential bridge without competing guest creation.
- Email account creation can create a Firebase user directly when no guest user exists.
- Player-facing auth errors no longer reveal capacitor://localhost, raw SDK messages or internal origin details.
- Settings preserves the active screen and does not switch an in-progress level to the menu/logo state.
- The Settings gear stops the pointer event before it can reach adjacent navigation controls.
- Native Settings removes expensive backdrop blur and native service-worker/cache work is disabled.
- In-game Dr. E and the speech bubble are positioned above the iPhone safe area and bottom navigation.
- The Account & Players title is kept clear of the close button.
- Native Google CocoaPods dependency, URL scheme, bundled GoogleService-Info.plist and Apple entitlement remain enabled.

STATIC/RENDER STATUS:
- JavaScript, shell, Python, JSON and plist checks pass.
- 45 game raster images plus the App Store icon decode successfully.
- 24 audio files parse successfully.
- 11 Dr. E poses remain 468 x 520 without aspect-ratio changes.
- Native layout harnesses at 320x568, 375x667, 390x844 and 393x852 show the dialogue above the bottom dock.

LIMITATION:
- Xcode signing and IPA creation require Codemagic/macOS.
- Google, Apple, email, keyboard, safe-area, Settings-resume, audio and touch behavior require a fresh TestFlight build for final device confirmation.
- This package is not a full campaign solvability audit.
