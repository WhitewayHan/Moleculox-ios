Moleculox — Player Name Rules

Last updated: 21 August 2026.

Your display name is public when you publish a score to a Career or Duel ranking, where any player can see it.

Names must not

  • Be offensive, hateful, or sexual
  • Harass, threaten, or target another person
  • Impersonate wHiTeWaY studio, game staff, or another player ("admin", "moderator", "support", etc.)
  • Contain links, phone numbers, or advertising
  • Contain HTML, scripts, or control characters
  • Be blank, or made only of repeated/invisible characters

What happens if a name breaks these rules

The game limits names to 18 characters, safely escapes displayed text, and rejects a conservative set of clearly inappropriate terms. Automated filtering cannot catch every variation; names that break these rules may also be reset or removed after review.

Reporting a name

On a public Career or Duel ranking, tap the ⋯ button beside the player and choose Report & Block. Sending a report requires a connected account; local blocking is also available in guest mode and hides that ranking profile on the current device. If in-app reporting is unavailable, email moleculoxsupport@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed and the reporter's identity is not shared with the reported player.

Changing your name

You can change your display name at any time from the Hall of Fame screen. Your score, stars, and rank are not affected by a name change.
