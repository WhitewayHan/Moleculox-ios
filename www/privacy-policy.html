Moleculox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026. Studio: wHiTeWaY studio (Orhan Akyol).

Moleculox ("the game") is developed by wHiTeWaY studio. This page explains what data the game processes and why.

What we collect

  • A Firebase account identifier. The game starts with an anonymous Firebase account. If you choose Apple, Google, or email/password sign-in, Firebase also processes the email address and basic account details needed for that sign-in.
  • Your chosen public display name (up to 18 characters), used for cloud profiles, friendly online Duels and public leaderboards where applicable.
  • Gameplay progress needed for local and optional cloud save: unlocked levels, stars, MoleCoins, achievements, research points and similar statistics.
  • Friendly online Duel data. When you create, join or search for a Duel, Firebase temporarily processes the room code, anonymous/account identifier, a per-device Duel client identifier, player names, round/turn state, board and move state, preset-message keys, presence timestamps, connection/reconnect state and match result. There is no free-text chat.
  • Basic usage analytics through Firebase Analytics on supported platforms, to understand aggregated play patterns. Analytics availability never blocks the game from starting.
  • App integrity signals on supported Android and iOS builds. Firebase App Check may process an app/device attestation token to help distinguish authentic app installations from unauthorized clients.
  • Player-name safety reports. A signed-in player may report a public display name. The report contains the reporter account identifier, the reported ranking/profile identifiers and name, the source ranking, app build, status, and timestamps. Reports are not public and are scheduled to expire after approximately 180 days.

What we do not request

We do not request your phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. An email address is processed only when you voluntarily use Apple, Google, or email/password sign-in.

Public leaderboard data

If you connect a permanent Apple, Google, or email account, your chosen display name and selected game statistics may be visible to other players on public Career and Duel rankings. Guest accounts can view rankings but are not published. Players can report a displayed name and block that ranking profile on their device. Room-code Duels are unranked; Quick Match may affect Duel Points, but neither mode changes campaign progress or Career Ranking results. See our Player Name Rules.

Temporary Duel data and retention

Online Duel rooms are designed to expire approximately six hours after creation, Quick Match queue tickets approximately two minutes after creation, friend invite tickets approximately fifteen minutes after creation, and player-name reports approximately 180 days after creation. Temporary records may remain briefly until the service completes scheduled cleanup.

Where data is stored

Cloud save, account, rankings, online Duel, app-integrity checks, safety reports, and supported analytics use Google Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, App Check, and Analytics). See Google's Privacy Policy.

Offline play

The campaign can be played offline. Progress made only in offline/local play remains in the device's local storage unless you use cloud-save features after connecting.

Data deletion

You can delete one cloud profile from Settings → Delete Cloud Data, or delete the complete Firebase account, cloud profiles, associated ranking entries, and safety reports authored by that account from the profile icon → Delete Account & All Data. Temporary records also expire through their retention schedule. See our Data Deletion page.

Children

Moleculox does not knowingly request personal information from children beyond the limited account, gameplay and temporary online data described above.
