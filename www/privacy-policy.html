Moleculox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026. Studio: wHiTeWaY studio (Orhan Akyol).

Moleculox ("the game") is developed by wHiTeWaY studio. This page explains what data the game processes and why.

What we collect

  • A Firebase account identifier. The game starts with an anonymous Firebase account. If you choose Google or email/password sign-in, Firebase also processes the email address and basic account details needed for that sign-in.
  • Your chosen public display name (up to 18 characters), used for cloud profiles, friendly online Duels and public leaderboards where applicable.
  • Gameplay progress needed for local and optional cloud save: unlocked levels, stars, MoleCoins, achievements, research points and similar statistics.
  • Friendly online Duel data. When you create, join or search for a Duel, Firebase temporarily processes the room code, anonymous/account identifier, a per-device Duel client identifier, player names, round/turn state, board and move state, preset-message keys, presence timestamps, connection/reconnect state and match result. There is no free-text chat.
  • Basic usage analytics through Firebase Analytics on supported platforms, to understand aggregated play patterns. Analytics availability never blocks the game from starting.

What we do not request

We do not request your phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. An email address is processed only when you voluntarily use Google or email/password sign-in.

Public leaderboard data

If you connect a permanent Google or email account, your chosen display name and selected game statistics may be visible to other players on the World Ranking. Guest accounts can view the ranking but are not published. Friendly online Duels are unranked and do not change campaign progress or World Ranking results. See our Player Name Rules.

Temporary Duel data and retention

Online Duel rooms are designed to expire approximately six hours after creation, and Quick Match queue tickets approximately two minutes after creation. Production deployment must enable Firestore Time-to-Live (TTL) cleanup for the expiresAt fields in the duelRooms and duelMatchQueue collections. Temporary records may remain until Firebase completes that scheduled cleanup.

Where data is stored

Cloud save, account, World Ranking, online Duel and supported analytics data use Google Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore and Analytics). See Google's Privacy Policy.

Offline play

The campaign can be played offline. Progress made only in offline/local play remains in the device's local storage unless you use cloud-save features after connecting.

Data deletion

You can delete one cloud profile from Settings → Delete Cloud Data, or delete the complete Firebase account, cloud profiles and associated World Ranking entries from the profile icon → Delete Account & All Data. Temporary Duel records expire through their retention schedule. See our Data Deletion page.

Children

Moleculox does not knowingly request personal information from children beyond the limited account, gameplay and temporary online data described above.
