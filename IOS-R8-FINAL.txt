APPLE AUTH
- A fresh raw nonce is generated for every Apple attempt.
- The exact raw nonce is supplied to native iOS.
- Native sends SHA-256(raw nonce) to Apple.
- Native returns bridge-safe top-level rawNonce, Apple ID token and authorization code.
- Firebase JS receives the same raw nonce plus the fresh Apple token and authorization code.
- No credential or nonce is reused.
